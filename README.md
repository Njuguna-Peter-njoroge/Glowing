# Glowing - Reveal The Beauty of Skin


A beautifully designed e-commerce website template focused on skincare and beauty products. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, Glowing offers a clean, modern, and user-friendly experience to showcase and sell your skincare collection.



## Features

- **Clean and Modern Design:** A visually appealing and intuitive layout that highlights your products.
- **Responsive Design:** Fully responsive and optimized for various screen sizes (desktop, tablet, mobile).
- **Hero Section:** Engaging hero sections with image carousels to showcase featured products or promotions.
- **New Arrivals & Best Sellers Sections:** Dedicated sections to display the latest and most popular products.
- **Product Listing:** Attractive product cards with images, prices, ratings, and quick action buttons (wishlist, cart).
- **Category Showcase:** Collection sections to categorize products (Face, Body, Hair).
- **Banner Promotions:** Eye-catching banner sections for special offers and highlights.
- **Feature Highlights:** Section to showcase key benefits and features of your brand or products.
- **Limited-Time Offer:** Engaging offer banner with a countdown timer to create urgency.
- **Blog Section:** Dedicated section to share skincare tips, news, and product information.
- **Footer with Newsletter:** Comprehensive footer with contact information, links, social media, and a newsletter signup form.
- **Mobile Navigation:** Smooth and user-friendly mobile navigation with a sidebar menu.
- **Header with Search & Cart:** Functional header with a search bar, user account, wishlist, and shopping cart previews.
- **Back to Top Button:** Convenient button for easy navigation back to the top of the page.
- **Scroll Reveal Effects:** Subtle animations on sections as the user scrolls, enhancing the visual experience.
